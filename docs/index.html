One method, shared across the tools.

  1. 01 · MEASURE

    On sealed public records

    Each tool measures every tier on the repository's own sealed public records (hashed, then frozen: its content hash is checked before a figure is shown): 1,000 held-out records for the reader, 60 + 60 pairs, 42 + 42 cases, 84 files. Intervals on every rate.

    1,000 records · 120 for the generative tiers
  2. 02 · SEAL

    Hashed, then frozen

    Each record carries its content hash. A page checks it before a figure is shown; a record that changed after sealing is not used.

    content hash dbf26abe… (reader)
  3. 03 · RERUN

    On your own files, at your desk

    One command reruns the whole sweep on your records. The report is written next to your file, and no data leaves the network.

    run it yourself
    git clone https://github.com/ArslaneSempai-ui/cascade-routingnpm ci --ignore-scripts npm run measure:yours -- --cases=your-file.csv the report is written next to your file, and nowhere else
    3 commands, no account, no upload
  4. 04 · DOSSIER

    Signed, current, verifiable

    Four reports against five controls: present, sealed, signed, fresh, consistent. A reviewer verifies the dossier without us, from the hashes and signatures.

    4 of 4 reports · 5 controls
reports present: 4 of 4consistent control: 4 of 4

A reviewer does not want four files. The Dossier reads all four public records and reports on them as one. In this Dossier, all four reports are present, and all four hold the top control, consistent.
