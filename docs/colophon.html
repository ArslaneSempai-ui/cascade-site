Who publishes this, under which licences, served from where, and what this site does to its visitors, which is nothing. The one page of a sales site that is usually mumbled; here it follows the same rule as the rest: plain, sourced, short.

Who publishes this

The objects on the appendix pages were modelled for this site by its author.

Where it is served

Static files, from GitHub Pages, out of the public repository. No server code of ours runs to show you this page.

What this site does to visitors: nothing

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no forms. The scripts on this site do two things only: the main screen synchronises its animation clock and tab title, and the appendix pages open their sections when you print. Switch them off and everything still works. Every figure displayed carries a declared source; the full declaration file is public, beside this page: releve.json, and the seal in the masthead is the content fingerprint of the sealed record the figures come from.

When we are wrong

The tool publishes its own errors: a retraction file lists every published conclusion that turned out to be wrong, held open by a test. This site inherits the rule: a corrected figure is corrected in daylight, not swapped quietly.
